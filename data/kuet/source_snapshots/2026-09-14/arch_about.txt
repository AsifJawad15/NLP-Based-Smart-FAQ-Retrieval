ARCH | KUET Department of Architecture The Department of Architecture at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) stands as one of the pioneering centers of architectural education in Bangladesh. Established with a commitment to nurturing creative, technically sound, and socially conscious architects, the department has consistently contributed to shaping the built environment of the nation. Situated in the vibrant city of Khulna the gateway to the Sundarbans - the department draws inspiration from its rich natural and cultural surroundings. This unique context fosters a design culture that balances contemporary architectural thinking with sensitivity to regional identity, climate, and heritage. Vision To be a center of excellence in architectural education, research, and practice by producing graduates who are globally competitive, ethically grounded, and capable of addressing the complex challenges of the built environment in Bangladesh and beyond. Mission To provide a rigorous and inspiring academic environment where students develop design proficiency, critical thinking, and professional competence, while embracing sustainability, cultural sensitivity, and technological innovation in all aspects of architectural practice Academic Program The department offers a five-year professional Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) degree program, affiliated with the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh. The curriculum is meticulously designed to integrate design studio education with technical, theoretical, and humanities-based knowledge. Students engage in studio-based design projects from the very first year, progressing through increasingly complex design challenges that culminate in an independent thesis project in the final year. The program also encompasses subjects such as architectural history and theory, structural systems, building technology, environmental design, urban design, and professional practice by ensuring a well-rounded architectural education. Why Choose Us Studio Culture A vibrant design studio environment that fosters creativity, peer learning, and architectural thinking from day one. Contextual Design Deep engagement with local climate, culture, and ecology, producing architecturally responsive and sustainable designs. Research & Innovation Active research programs addressing vernacular architecture, sustainable building, urban resilience, and climate-adaptive design. Global Perspective Exposure to international architectural discourse, design competitions, and exchange opportunities. Expert Faculty A dedicated team of experienced educators and practicing architects committed to academic and professional excellence. Industry Linkage Strong ties with architectural firms, government bodies, and NGOs providing practical exposure and internship opportunities.
