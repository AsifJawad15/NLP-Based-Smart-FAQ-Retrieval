IEM | KUET Department of Industrial Engineering and Management The Industrial Engineering & Management (IEM) department at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) has established itself as a prominent and internationally respected engineering discipline. Renowned for its focus on practical applications in service and production sectors, the department began its journey in 2001 with postgraduate programs and expanded to include undergraduate programs in 2006. Starting with an initial intake of 30 undergraduate students, the department increased its capacity to 60 students from the 2009-2010 academic session, reflecting its growing recognition and demand. Today, the department offers a Bachelor of Science in Engineering (B.Sc. Eng.) in Industrial & Production Engineering (IPE) at the undergraduate level and a postgraduate degree in Industrial Engineering & Management (IEM). As an interdisciplinary field, industrial engineering integrates various elements of service and production, emphasizing the critical need to manage and balance changes within these environments. The discipline covers the analysis, design, installation, control, evaluation, and improvement of complex systems, all while ensuring the preservation of human, social, and environmental health. A clear vision guides the IEM department: to nurture the next generation of leaders who will propel Bangladesh's industrial sector to a globally competitive level, playing a crucial role in the nation's journey towards becoming a developed country. To realize this vision, the department has designed a curriculum that not only strengthens students' technical knowledge but also enhances their leadership skills and creativity. This comprehensive approach ensures that graduates are not just proficient engineers but also effective communicators and leaders, equipped to guide and innovate within the industrial sector. Moreover, the department provides an outstanding research environment, complemented by high-quality teaching, which enables students to excel academically and professionally. The emphasis on research has led to numerous undergraduate projects being published in globally recognized journals and conferences, further solidifying the department's reputation for academic excellence. In summary, the IEM department at KUET is dedicated to producing engineers who possess both technical expertise and leadership capabilities. These graduates are poised to play a pivotal role in transforming Bangladesh's industrial landscape, driving innovation, and contributing to the nation's economic development and global standing. Mission and Vision The mission of the Industrial Engineering & Management (IEM) department at KUET is to produce highly skilled engineers equipped with the technical expertise, leadership qualities, and innovative mindset needed to meet the demands of the modern industrial world. The department is committed to providing a comprehensive education that blends theoretical knowledge with practical application, fostering an environment where students can excel in both academic and professional settings. The vision of the IEM department is to cultivate a new generation of leaders who will drive Bangladesh's industrial sector towards global competitiveness, contributing significantly to the country's advancement into the ranks of developed nations. By emphasizing excellence in education, research, and leadership development, the IEM department aims to empower its graduates to lead transformative changes within the industrial sector, ultimately enhancing the nation's economic growth and global standing. Program Objectives The Department of Industrial Engineering & Management (IEM) at KUET dedicates itself to providing students with the essential knowledge, skills, and resources necessary for successful careers in industrial engineering, management, and related fields. We offer an academic program that helps students choose the educational path best suited to their personal and professional goals. Our goal is to create a comprehensive and adaptable learning environment that promotes academic excellence and prepares graduates to thrive in a dynamic and changing industrial landscape. We are also committed to offering advanced education and research opportunities that empower graduate students to become leaders in industrial engineering, management, and related fields. Our graduate programs are tailored to provide specialized knowledge and research skills, helping students achieve academic and professional excellence. We strive to create a rigorous and innovative learning environment that prepares graduates with the expertise necessary to address complex challenges in a fast-evolving industrial landscape and to contribute meaningfully to the progress of their chosen fields.
