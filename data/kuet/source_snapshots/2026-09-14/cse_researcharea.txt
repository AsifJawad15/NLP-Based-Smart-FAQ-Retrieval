CSE | KUET Research Area Research in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) is structured to support advanced knowledge creation, postgraduate education, and national development priorities. The department conducts both theoretical and applied research in core and emerging areas of computing, with a strong emphasis on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Key research areas include deep learning, multimodal and federated learning, swarm intelligence, evolutionary computation, soft computing, pattern recognition, optimization, game theory, and Natural Language Processing, including text and sentiment mining, neural machine translation, and large language models. Research in Data Science and Computational Systems focuses on large-scale data analytics, text and data mining, tensor computation, data cube computation, incomplete and uncertain data processing, query processing algorithms, and large-scale data management supported by GPU computing. Systems-oriented research includes cloud and fog computing, distributed systems, trust and security in cloud environments, Internet of Things (IoT), and performance analysis of networked systems, addressing scalability, efficiency, and reliability requirements of modern computing infrastructures. The department has strong research activities in Cybersecurity and Intelligent Systems, encompassing applied cryptography, information security protocols, blockchain technologies, and data and image encryption. Research in computer vision and image processing covers image analysis and understanding, deep vision, visual modeling, foreground–background modeling, and human activity estimation. Hardware- and reliability-oriented research addresses soft error tolerance, hardware redundancy, error correction coding, embedded systems, and hardware–software co-design to ensure dependable and resilient computing systems. Interdisciplinary and application-driven research integrates CSE with healthcare, robotics, and smart technologies, including bioinformatics, biomedical signal processing, medical informatics, AI for health, autonomous and humanitarian robotics, and extended reality technologies. Research activities support postgraduate supervision, scholarly publications, and innovation through experimental validation using simulations, benchmarks, real-world datasets, and prototype development. The research outcomes contribute to academic excellence, quality assurance objectives, industry relevance, and societal impact in alignment with national and international accreditation standards.
