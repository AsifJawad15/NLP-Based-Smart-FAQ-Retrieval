HUM | KUET Department of Humanities and Business Welcome to the Department of Humanities and Business Academic Coverage Across KUET The Department of Humanities and Business currently provides academic instruction to undergraduate students across a broad range of disciplines under three major faculties of KUET. Under the Faculty of Civil Engineering , the Department provides courses to students of Civil Engineering, Urban and Regional Planning, Building Engineering and Construction Management, and Architecture. Under the Faculty of Electrical and Electronic Engineering , its academic coverage extends to Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, and Materials Science and Engineering. Under the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering , the Department provides instruction to students of Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering and Management, Energy Science and Engineering, Leather Engineering, Textile Engineering, Chemical Engineering, and Mechatronics Engineering. Through this extensive academic coverage, the Department serves students from diverse engineering, architecture, and planning disciplines across KUET. The courses offered by the Department are distributed across different academic levels according to the requirements of the respective undergraduate programmes. The contact hours, course contents, and academic emphasis are determined in accordance with the curricular needs of the respective disciplines. Major Academic Disciplines The Department's teaching and academic activities encompass five major areas: Accounting, Economics, Management, Sociology, English. Together, these disciplines provide students with a broad academic foundation that complements their technical and professional education. Contribution to Engineering Education The Department of Humanities and Business occupies a unique and strategically important position in KUET's engineering education system. Engineering education today extends far beyond technical knowledge and problem-solving. Engineers and other technical professionals increasingly work in complex organizational, economic, social, environmental, and institutional settings where professional success requires an understanding of both technical and human dimensions. In this context, the Department serves as an important bridge between technical expertise and real-world professional practice. Through courses in accounting, economics, management, sociology, and English, the Department helps engineering and architecture students understand how technical decisions interact with business operations, financial resources, markets, organizations, public policy, society, communication, ethics, and human behaviour. Accounting and business-related education develops students' understanding of financial decision-making, cost management, budgeting, investment, and organizational performance. Economics enables them to appreciate markets, resource allocation, economic policy, development, and the broader economic consequences of technical decisions. Management prepares students to understand organizations, leadership, entrepreneurship, project management, human resources, and strategic decision-making. Sociology introduces the social and institutional dimensions of development and technological change, while English strengthens students' communication, professional writing, presentation, and academic skills. This interdisciplinary contribution is particularly significant because many KUET graduates eventually become engineers, project managers, entrepreneurs, consultants, researchers, administrators, policymakers, and organizational leaders. Their professional responsibilities therefore require not only technical competence but also the ability to communicate effectively, manage resources, understand organizations, evaluate economic consequences, and respond responsibly to societal needs. Thus, the Department of Humanities and Business contributes to the development of well-rounded engineering professionals by complementing KUET's strong technical education with the knowledge, skills, and perspectives necessary for responsible professional practice and leadership. The Department's role reflects the broader principle that excellent engineering is not solely about designing technically sound solutions; it is also about understanding the economic, organizational, social, ethical, and human contexts in which those solutions are implemented. Future Academic Development The Department is also preparing for further academic expansion. As part of its future development, the Department expects to introduce Master of Philosophy (M.Phil.) and Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) programmes, creating opportunities for advanced study and research in relevant areas of humanities, social sciences, business, economics, accounting, management, and interdisciplinary fields. The proposed expansion is expected to strengthen the Department's contribution to research, postgraduate education, interdisciplinary scholarship, and evidence-based solutions to contemporary economic, business, social, and organizational challenges.
