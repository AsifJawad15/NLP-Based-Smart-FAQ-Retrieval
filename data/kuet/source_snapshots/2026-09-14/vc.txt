                            Bangladesh offered me to lead Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) as its
                            Vice-Chancellor. Having been a part of this institution for many years, I find it a great
                            privilege to serve the university, which means so much to me. At KUET, extraordinary things are happening every day. My goal as Vice-Chancellor is to ensure
                            that this momentum continues. I am committed to providing the support and resources our
                            students, faculty, and staff require to thrive in academics, leadership, research, and service.
                            Together, we share the responsibility of shaping individuals distinguished by their honor and
                            commitment to service. I recognize that past events may have brought challenges and concerns. However, these challenges
                            do not define us as a university, nor do they diminish the remarkable achievements realized here
                            every day. They serve as a reminder that our dedication to core values is not static rather a
                            continuous journey. KUET is an institution that flourishes on diversity, inclusivity, and
                            respect for all voices, and I am fully committed to strengthening these principles. As we move
                            forward together, I have faith in the strength of our community and our shared commitment to
                            excellence.
                            I eagerly look forward to collaborating with each of you to uphold KUET’s proud legacy and
                            contribute to the development of our country. Thank you. Prof. Mohammad Mashud, Ph.D. Vice Chancellor Vice-Chancellor
