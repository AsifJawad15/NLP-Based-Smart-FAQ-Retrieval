CSE | KUET Message From Head Dr. Al-Mahmud Welcome to the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). The department started its academic activities from 26th September 1999. Since then, it is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation to prepare students for the challenges of a rapidly evolving technological world. Our academic programs are designed to provide strong theoretical foundations along with practical skills in computing, software development, artificial intelligence, data science, networks, and hardware systems. We emphasize research-oriented learning, problem-solving ability, and ethical responsibility among our students. The department is supported by a team of dedicated and experienced faculty members who are actively involved in teaching, research, and professional development. Modern laboratories, industry-relevant curricula, and collaborative projects help our students gain hands-on experience. We encourage participation in research, competitions, internships, and community-oriented activities to foster creativity and leadership. Our graduates are well-equipped for higher studies, research careers, and professional success in industry. We continuously strive to improve academic quality through curriculum updates and industry–academia collaboration. We invite students, researchers, and industry partners to explore our department and be part of our journey toward academic excellence and technological advancement. Prof. Dr. Al-Mahmud Head Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Khulna-9203
