KUET | Khulna University of Engineering & Technology Home News Details "Walton Smart Fridge Presents BizBattle: Innovation In Tech,"  National Idea Case Competition, by KUET Career Club The National Idea Case Competition , titled "Walton Smart Fridge Presents BizBattle: Innovation In Tech," was organized for the first time by KUET Career Club at Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET) to foster students' intellect, creativity, and innovative technological thinking. The two-month-long competition began on November 14 last year and concluded with the Grand Finale on January 17. Out of 167 teams from 50 leading universities across the country, 10 teams were selected for the Grand Finale through two rounds of selection. The Grand Finale was held on the evening of January 17 at the university auditorium, with Vice-Chancellor Prof. Dr. Muhammad Masud as the chief guest. Special guests included Pro Vice-Chancellor Prof. Dr. Sheikh Shariful Alam, Director (Student Welfare) Prof. Dr. Mohammad Sultan Mahmud, and KUET Career Club Co-Moderator Md. Al Imran Fahim. The event was presided over by Prof. Dr. Ismail Saifullah. During the event, participating students presented various ideas on technology, environment, safety management, business, waste recycling, and product development. Contestants faced questions from the panel of judges during the idea presentation round. Vice-Chancellor Prof. Dr. Muhammad Masud handed over prizes to the winners, with Team Phoenixishia securing first place, Team Wrong Direction coming second, and Team Civic finishing third.
