ECE | KUET Message From Head Prof. Dr. Monir Hossen, SMIEEE, FIEB Welcome to the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE), where we nurture future Electronics & Communication Engineers and responsible citizens. It is both an honor and a pleasure to present this department, a leading entity at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) in Bangladesh, established in 2001. Over the years, it has gained widespread recognition for its outstanding teaching and research capabilities. Our primary objective is to provide a world-class education to talented youth, empowering them to contribute meaningfully to the development of society. The commitment and dedication of our faculty members create an exceptional learning environment. Here, cutting- edge laboratories, well-equipped libraries, abundant computer resources, extracurricular activities, and, most importantly, personalized care converge. The department is devoted to both theoretical and applied research, addressing challenges in Electronics, Solid State Devices, and Power Electronics, VLSI and Nano Technology, Signal and Image Processing, Wireless Communications, Multimedia Communications and Networking, Biomedical Engineering, Antenna Design, Optical devices and Communication, Broadband Mobile Communications, Machine Learning and Internet of Things (IoTs). A friendly and congenial atmosphere prevails, fostering healthy teacher-student relationships. Students are encouraged to excel in today's dynamic research and job landscapes. Our curriculum is regularly updated to incorporate the latest advancements in Electronics & Communication Engineering, ensuring our graduates are well-prepared to tackle emerging challenges in both research and industry. Notably, alumni from this department have established a distinguished reputation in the global professional arena. Prof. Dr. Monir Hossen, SMIEEE, FIEB Head, Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, KUET, Khulna-9203, Bangladesh
